There is a quiet kind of love that asks for nothing extraordinary. It lives in the hours we steal from sleep, in conversations that wander without purpose, in the familiar warmth of someone who has learned every version of us and chooses to stay. To be loved so completely is to be known beyond the carefully arranged parts of ourselves, and to discover that even our imperfections have a place to rest.

Perhaps devotion is not found in grand promises, but in the tenderness of ordinary days. In making a little more time when there is none to spare, in listening when words become difficult, in finding a thousand small reasons to remain. And if love is a choice we make again and again, then I would choose you in every quiet, unremarkable moment, until the ordinary becomes something sacred.

How to
  1. Highlight. Drag across any words. The style with the dark border is applied when you let go.
  2. Change style. Click a style above.
  3. Layer. Highlight the same words again. Fills get deeper with each pass, and different styles combine, like Soft with Box for a filled box.
  4. Remove. Click a highlight to remove the top layer, press ⌘Z to undo the last one, or use Remove all.
  5. Use it on your site. Copy JS and Copy CSS, add both to your page, and start it on the element that holds your text: 
    const hl = enableHighlight(document.querySelector('#article'), 'hl-marker');
